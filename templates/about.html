About WasteVision AI

WasteVision AI is an AI-powered biomedical waste classification platform designed to automate healthcare waste segregation using Deep Learning. The system identifies medical waste items in real-time through image upload and live camera detection, helping hospitals improve safety, reduce human error, and ensure proper waste disposal.

Project Features

Live Detection

Detect biomedical waste instantly using a live camera feed.

Image Upload

Upload waste images and receive AI-based classification results.

Detection History

Track and manage previous predictions with timestamps.

Safe Segregation

Promotes safer handling and disposal of medical waste.
