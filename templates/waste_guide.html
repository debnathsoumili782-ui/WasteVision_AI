Waste Segregation Guide

Proper biomedical waste segregation reduces infection risks, protects healthcare workers, and ensures environmentally safe disposal. Follow these guidelines when handling hospital waste.

Waste Categories

Unused Syringe

Unused syringes should be stored in a sterile environment. Do not mix with contaminated waste. Keep sealed until required for medical use.

Waste Syringe

Used syringes are considered infectious biomedical waste. Immediately dispose into approved sharps containers. Never recap or reuse needles.

Unused Tablets

Unused medicines should remain in original packaging and be stored according to manufacturer instructions. Avoid contamination and moisture exposure.

Waste Tablets

Expired or damaged medicines must be disposed through authorized pharmaceutical waste systems and never mixed with regular household garbage.

Color Coding System

Infectious Waste

Red Bin

Blue Bin

Safety Guidelines

• Always wear gloves while handling biomedical waste.

• Never mix infectious and non-infectious waste.

• Dispose sharps immediately after use.

• Follow hospital and biomedical waste management regulations.

• Use designated color-coded bins for proper segregation.
